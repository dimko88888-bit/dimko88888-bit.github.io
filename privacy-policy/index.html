Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: 23 September 2026

Sudoku Journey does not require an account. Game progress and preferences are stored on the device. The free version may use Google AdMob for advertising, and an optional one-time Google Play purchase removes normal ads.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the Sudoku Journey Android application, package com.sudokujourney.android (the “App”). It explains what information is handled by the App, why it is handled, and the choices available to users.

2. Information stored locally

The App stores the following information locally on the device so its features work:

  • saved Sudoku puzzles, elapsed time and gameplay state;
  • Journey progress, unlocked content, album progress and statistics;
  • audio and gameplay preferences;
  • advertising display cadence; and
  • a limited cached Remove Ads entitlement used to avoid showing ads while Google Play ownership is being restored.

The developer does not operate an account system and does not directly request a user's name, postal address, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise location through the App. Depending on Android and the user's device settings, local application data may be included in Android's system backup service.

3. Advertising

The free version may display banner and interstitial advertising through the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). Google and its advertising partners may process information such as IP address, device and advertising identifiers, general device information, ad interactions and diagnostic information to provide, measure, secure and improve ads. The precise data processed depends on the user's region, device settings and consent choices.

Where required, the App requests or refreshes advertising consent before requesting eligible ads and provides access to privacy and cookie choices. Purchasing Remove Ads stops the App from requesting or displaying normal banner and interstitial ads. It does not change information independently processed by Google Play for the purchase itself.

Learn more in Google's partner data explanation and Privacy Policy.

4. Google Play Billing

The optional lifetime Remove Ads purchase is processed by Google Play. Google handles payment details. The App receives limited purchase information, such as the product, purchase state and purchase token, as necessary to verify ownership, acknowledge the transaction and restore the entitlement. The App does not receive or store full credit card or bank account details. The purchase is not consumed and is intended to remain associated with the owning Google account, subject to Google Play's rules and availability.

5. Sharing and sale of information

The developer does not sell personal information. Information may be processed by Google through Google Play services, Google Play Billing, Android backup and Google AdMob as described above. No separate third-party analytics or production crash-reporting service is currently used by the App.

6. Retention and deletion

Local gameplay data remains on the device until it is cleared from the App's Settings, cleared through Android system settings, or the App is uninstalled. An uninstall or system-level clear removes local App data, subject to any Android backup configured by the user. Google Play purchase records and information processed by Google are controlled by Google and the user's Google account settings. A valid Remove Ads purchase may be restored after reinstalling the App.

7. User choices

  • Use the App's Settings to clear saved puzzles, Journey progress, album data and statistics.
  • Use Android settings to clear all local App storage or uninstall the App.
  • Use “Privacy and cookie settings” in the App when shown to review available advertising choices.
  • Use Google account and Android privacy settings to manage Google-controlled data.
  • Purchase Remove Ads to stop normal ads from being requested and displayed by the App.

8. Children

The App does not knowingly ask children to submit names, contact details or precise location. Advertising configuration and consent treatment must follow the target audience selections made for the App in Google Play and AdMob. A parent or guardian may clear all locally stored data using Android settings or by uninstalling the App.

9. Security

The App limits its own storage to information needed for gameplay, settings, advertising cadence and purchase entitlement. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and third-party processing is governed by the security practices of the relevant provider.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the App's features, service providers or legal obligations change. The date at the top of this page identifies the latest published version.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email kontakt@dimitrioskorakis.com. Do not include payment card information in a support request; payments are handled by Google Play.
